# INK-A-BOT  | Smart Air Filtration and Eco-Ink Research Robot

INK-A-BOT is an environmental robotics project that explores mobile air-pollution monitoring, multi-stage filtration and the potential recovery of carbon-rich particulate matter for sustainable ink development.

The system integrates an Arduino UNO, PM2.5 filter, HEPA filter, activated-carbon filter, gas sensor, airflow detection sensor, ultrasonic sensor and LED display.

### Core Working Process
1. Polluted air intake through a blower.
2. PM2.5 particulate filtration.
3. HEPA-based fine-particle filtration.
4. Activated-carbon adsorption of selected gaseous contaminants.
5. Airflow and air-quality monitoring.
6. Controlled collection and laboratory evaluation of captured particulate material.
7. Investigation of safe material recovery and experimental eco-ink development.

### Project Objectives
- Explore mobile air-pollution collection.
- Implement multi-stage filtration.
- Monitor environmental conditions using sensors.
- Investigate potential reuse of captured carbon-rich particles.
- Promote sustainable engineering and circular-economy principles.

**Our Vision:** From Pollution to Possibility – From Soot to Ink.

*Note: Eco-ink production is a research objective and requires laboratory validation of the recovered material.*
